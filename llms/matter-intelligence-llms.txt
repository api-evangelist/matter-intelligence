# Matter Intelligence

> Matter Intelligence builds ultraspectral imaging sensors — roughly 2,000 spectral bands from deep
> ultraviolet through thermal infrared — and Large Geospatial Models that reason over the data, for
> satellites, aircraft, drones and robots. Founded in 2024 by former NASA JPL engineers Vishnu Sridhar
> and Thomas Chrien with former Caltech scientist Nathan Stein. Its first satellite, EARTH-1, is
> intended to deliver sub-meter hyperspectral and thermal imaging.
